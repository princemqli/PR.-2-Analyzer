

##  Project Overview

 Analyzer is an Excel-based sales data analysis project created to analyze sales data and present useful business insights through calculations, analysis tables, charts, and a dashboard.

##  Dataset

The dataset contains the following fields:

* Customer ID
* Customer Name
* Region
* Product Category
* Sales
* Quantity
* Discount
* Date
* Profit

##  Analysis Performed

### 1. Descriptive Statistics

The project includes:

* Count
* Mean
* Standard Deviation
* Minimum
* Maximum
* Sum

### 2. Customer Analysis

Customer analysis includes:

* Customer sales summary
* Top 10 Customers by Sales

### 3. Monthly Analysis

Monthly sales were analyzed to understand sales trends over time.

### 4. Region Analysis

Sales were analyzed across different regions.

### 5. Product Category Analysis

Sales were analyzed according to product categories.

##  Dashboard

The project includes a Professional Sales Performance Dashboard containing:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Average Discount
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales by Region
* Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Sales by Product Category
* Key Insights

##  Charts Used

* Line Chart — Monthly Sales Trend
* Column Chart — Sales by Region
* Bar Chart — Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Bar Chart — Sales by Product Category

##  Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

* Monthly changes in sales performance
* Regional sales performance
* High-value customers
* Product category sales performance
* Overall sales, profit, order volume, and discount metrics
